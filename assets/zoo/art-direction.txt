NATTNYCKELN — ART DIRECTION AND IMAGE PROMPTS

Production assets: forest.webp, wetland.webp, greenhouse.webp, animals.webp.
Created with the built-in image generation tool. Scene backgrounds are 1536 x
1024; the final transparent animal atlas is 1448 x 1086, four columns by three
rows. WebP copies preserve the generated compositions and alpha transparency.
The portable app embeds all four files. There are no runtime image-generation
requests or image-service dependencies.

SHARED SCENE PROMPT

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: richly hand-painted background art
for a sophisticated hidden-object point-and-click escape-room game for an adult
learning Swedish, inspired by classic Japanese animated feature-film backgrounds
and Nordic storybook illustration. Landscape 3:2 image, no frames, no UI, no
lettering, no logos, no people and absolutely no animals. Intricate inspectable
details, thin ink drawings under watercolor/gouache, deliberate shapes, natural
perspective, cinematic composition, deep layered foreground, midground and
background. Swedish midsummer late twilight, blue spruce and pine greens,
indigo and petrol shadows, warm small amber lanterns, pale rose and lilac, moon
glow. Enchanting, inviting mystery, never horror, flat vector or generic clipart.
Provide convincing quiet shadowy nooks, dappled logs, rocks and vegetation for
separately composited hidden animals. No enormous empty central area.

ANIMAL ATLAS

Twelve individual naturalistic painterly animals on a truly transparent
background, strict four-column, three-row regular grid. Every animal centred
in its own equal cell with transparent padding, no overlap or cropped anatomy.
No scenery, labels, numbers, frames, ground or shadows outside the animals.
Fine watercolor/gouache texture, delicate dark ink contours, coherent twilight
lighting, restrained blue-green and grey shadow palette, tiny amber eyes.
Not emoji or flat vector.

Row 1: tawny owl; crouching Eurasian lynx with ear tufts; curled European
hedgehog; reclining red fox with a white-tipped curled tail.
Row 2: beaver with paddle tail; grey heron with long thin legs; moss-green frog;
low-swimming otter showing head and curved back.
Row 3: three-toed sloth hanging by both front claws, no branch; pale orchid-like
butterfly with violet markings; coiled olive snake with slightly raised head;
moss-green chameleon with tightly curled tail and gripping feet.

FINAL ATLAS EDIT PROMPT

Background removal only. Keep every animal, its position, size, packing and
exact anatomy unchanged. Remove the entire grey/white/cream paper background,
gradients, grain, ambient shadow patches and all shadows outside each animal.
The space between animals and the padding at all canvas edges must be completely
invisible transparent, zero opacity / alpha 0, not grey, translucent, white,
checkerboard or black. Make twelve clean-edged painterly cutouts floating on
fully transparent empty space. Do not redraw, move or resize the animals.
No halos and no additional effects.
